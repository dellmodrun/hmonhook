THREE PROTECTION LAYERS

How the layers connect.

Each layer protects a different decision.

BEFORE A TRADE

Compare developers

Same-name coins belong side by side. A 2–10% developer lock and option terms would be displayed next to coins that stay unlocked.

DURING A TRADE

Enforce limits

The trader chooses a maximum spend and minimum received amount. The Guard checks a supported transaction's final result.

AFTER A TRADE

Choose privacy

An optional Privacy Rings path could support confidential transfers without hiding the public evidence needed to verify launch claims.

VERIFY THE ACTUAL FEATURE

The current launch and market pages do not issue options, certify developer locks or execute private transfers. Those actions must be backed by deployed contracts and visible transaction evidence before a coin receives a protection label.

THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Why focus on the final result?

A quote is useful, but the amount a trade finally settles for is what matters to the person signing it.

A published study of hook-based exchanges found that programmable trading logic can affect what a trader pays or receives beyond the standard fee display. That study concerns EVM exchanges; it is background research, not a test or endorsement of h/monhook.

Our design response is to let a trader set limits before signing a supported route and check the resulting balances during settlement. The paper does not establish that our launchpad, issuer options or privacy ideas are safe or complete. Those require their own implementation and review.

Read the original paper and the technical scope

An Empirical Study of Undisclosed Charges in Hook-Based Decentralized Exchanges by Kai Ma, Tianle Sun, Shun Zhou, Boyao Yang, Ningyu He and Haoyu Wang, arXiv:2610.36682 (2026). It studies undisclosed settlement charges in hook-based DEXs on Ethereum and BNB Smart Chain.
