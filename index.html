HOW THE DEVELOPER OPTION IS DESIGNED

A developer locks 2–10% of a coin's supply and sells options against it. Unlocked coins remain visible with an unlocked label. Each option costs 10% of its strike price, with the strike following a launch bonding-curve model.

If the market price moves above the strike, an option holder can exercise and buy the locked tokens at that price. If it never reaches the strike, the premium is the holder's loss. A developer sale before expiry would forfeit the locked supply to option holders. Once the locked allocation is sold, collected premiums would fund market buybacks.

Option terms shown here describe the product design. An actual lock, option or forfeiture must be verified against its deployed contract before any coin receives a protection label.

FROM THE BUILDER

Why build h/monhook?

Launching a coin should be straightforward. Understanding what happens after launch should be just as clear. h/monhook brings the coin, its Mint, its market and the rules around a trade into one place.

You can use the launch and market tools now. Option and privacy actions will appear only when their contracts and interfaces can be verified.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Know what you are using.

Who creates the coin?

Your connected wallet signs and pays for the Pump creation transaction. h/monhook prepares the form and metadata; it does not hold your wallet keys.

When will a price appear?

A verified creation is not a live trading market by itself. Price, liquidity and volume appear after a pool is active and a third-party indexer reports it.

Does launching here lock the developer's tokens?

No. A coin receives a protection label only when a lock and option contract can be verified. A standard coin creation does not lock the developer's tokens.

Are transfers private by default?

No. A standard launch and market trade remain public. Optional privacy requires its own supported transaction flow.

Where is the official h/monhook token address?

The official address is TBA. Check the full address here when it is published; do not rely on a ticker alone.
