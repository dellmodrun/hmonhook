FROM THE BUILDER

Why build h/monhook?

Launching a coin should be straightforward. Understanding what happens after launch should be just as clear. h/monhook brings the coin, its Mint, its market and the rules around a trade into one place.

I am building the deeper protection layer in public. You can use the launch and market tools now; issuer options and privacy will only be shown as live when their contracts and interfaces are ready.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Know what you are using.

Who creates the coin?

Your connected wallet signs and pays for the Pump creation transaction. h/monhook prepares the form and metadata; it does not hold your wallet keys.

When will a price appear?

A verified creation is not a live trading market by itself. Price, liquidity and volume appear after a pool is active and a third-party indexer reports it.

Does launching here lock the developer's tokens?

No. The issuer lock and options system is still in development. A coin launched here does not automatically receive that protection.

Where is the official h/monhook token address?

The official address is TBA. Check the full address here when it is published; do not rely on a ticker alone.
