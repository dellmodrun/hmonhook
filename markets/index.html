WHAT THE LABELS MEAN

What do these market labels mean?

A market can be live without being the official h/monhook token. h/monhook therefore never infers provenance from a token name, ticker or trading venue.

Official token identifies the published h/monhook CA. Independent launch identifies a separate coin with a confirmed mainnet creation receipt; it does not make that coin an official h/monhook asset. A published registry entry makes either record discoverable across devices.

Live market means a third-party indexer has observed a liquidity pool and trading data for the Mint. Price and volume are informational observations, not guarantees, endorsements or investment advice.

ISSUER PROTECTION IS NOT YET A MARKET LABEL

The proposed same-name comparison, verified developer locks and option coverage are not active in this directory. Current cards do not certify that an issuer has locked supply, sold options or passed a developer-sale check. Those labels will appear only when the underlying contracts and evidence can be verified.
