The default browser-compatible RPC is operated by PublicNode, not h/monhook or Solana. It receives ordinary RPC requests and the signed transaction when submitted. Production operators should replace it with their own trusted Solana RPC endpoint before launch.

ISSUER ACCOUNTABILITY / DESIGN STAGE

Let buyers compare the promise before they trade.

The issuer layer adapts the Vamend protection design to h/monhook launches. It compares same-name Mints and makes a developer's collateral and option terms inspectable before anyone relies on a protection label.

These are conditional rights, not a price guarantee. A buyer can lose the premium. A developer-sale violation needs independently verifiable evidence and a reviewed forfeiture rule; a lock by itself does not make the market safe.

01 / COMPARE PROPOSED

One name, every Mint

Keep same-name launches together. Show developer identity, Mint, lock percentage and the exact option terms so a familiar name cannot stand in for verified provenance.

02 / COMMIT PROPOSED

Lock and price the option

A developer would lock 2%–10% of supply. The proposed premium is 10% of the strike price per option; the strike follows a published Pump-style bonding-curve rule. Both formula and expiry need to be fixed before sale.

03 / SETTLE PROPOSED

Exercise or claim

If the market exceeds the strike, an eligible holder could exercise at the strike. A verified early developer sale could trigger collateral forfeiture. If the strike is not reached, the buyer risks the premium. Buybacks from premiums after the locked allocation sells out require a separate execution path.

NOT AN ACTIVE LAUNCH FEATURE

This page currently creates a token and compiles a JLM1 intent-policy plan. It does not lock issuer supply, sell options, detect developer sales, issue forfeiture claims or route option premiums into buybacks. Those steps need a production contract, reliable violation evidence, an independently reviewed settlement path and dedicated interfaces.

WHAT GETS CREATED

One launch, three protocol artifacts.

01 / TOKEN-2022 MINT

The asset and metadata created through the supported Pump route.

02 / MARKET ROUTE

The Pump create_v2 creation path, using SOL as the supported quote asset.

03 / DEFAULT INTENT POLICY

A local JLM1 policy proposal for future wallet or publisher integration.

The Mint is the token itself. The Pump route creates its initial trading path. The JLM1 policy selection is saved in the local plan, not registered on-chain by this launcher. A wallet could later display such a baseline, but only an integrated h/monhook Guard transaction enforces a trader's signed limits.

Issuer options and a protected intent are distinct: an option would cover a developer's publicly verifiable commitment, while an intent protects the outcome of one supported trade. Neither is automatically attached to every coin created here.

PRE-FLIGHT

Launch readiness checklist.

The software checks metadata and the selected Pump route. The creator must still review the source JSON, the wallet's final instruction and the amount of SOL required. This interface does not expose controls for mint or metadata authority configuration; inspect Pump's defaults before approving.

Required inputs

  • Metadata URI resolves to valid token metadata.
  • Name and symbol fit the selected launch route limits.
  • Creator wallet has sufficient SOL for rent and fees.
  • SOL quote asset and Pump create_v2 route are understood.

Creator decisions

  • Whether the verified metadata and social links are final.
  • Whether the connected wallet should pay the shown creation costs.
  • Whether to export the local JLM1 policy proposal for later integration.
  • Whether Pump's authority defaults meet the creator's requirements.
VERIFIABLE DEPENDENCIES

The launch path is tied to Pump’s published interface.

Users can review the exact program, route and instruction name inside the compiled launch plan before approving a mainnet transaction.

h/monhook uses Pump’s published create_v2 interface and checks that the assembled instruction targets 6EF8r…F6P, the Pump program address used on mainnet and devnet. The new Token-2022 Mint keypair is generated only in the browser, partially signs the creation transaction, and is never transmitted as a reusable secret. The connected wallet remains the payer and final transaction signer.

The compiler exposes the instruction discriminator, ordered accounts, selected quote asset, metadata URI and target program before signing. This keeps the dependency review inside the product instead of sending creators to an external code-hosting account.

FAQ / LAUNCH

Before creating a coin.

Does a default policy prevent users from adding stricter limits?

The selected default policy is a local proposal, not an enforced issuer rule. A trader using an integrated h/monhook Guard route can sign their own max-input, min-output and expiry bounds.

Is the policy tied to one liquidity pool?

No. h/monhook’s intent policy is evaluated around the chosen transaction route, allowing it to move across supported launchpads, routers and markets.

Who signs the launch transaction?

The connected creator wallet. h/monhook compiles the plan and exposes the exact output; it does not take custody of the creator’s signing authority.

Does launching here provide a developer lock or options?

No. The issuer protection model is a design proposal. The current launcher does not create a lock, option position or claim. Any future listing will show the verified contract and exact terms before a user can participate.
